# Nagare 0.11.0

Nagare 0.11.0 lets a person choose whether a protected-site sign-in outlives the browser. A
remembered sign-in lasts a year while the browser keeps using a protected site at least once
every 30 days; any other sign-in ends when the browser closes.

## Added

- **Keep me signed in.** The auth-portal contract carries an optional `remember` on password
  sign-in, the MFA step and passkey sign-in, and the portal forwards it to Shomei, which now
  records it on the session and reports `remembered` with every token pair
  (`mori://shinzui/shomei` commit `6179d567`, pinned in `cli/nagare-access/cabal.project`).
  `nagare-access` gives a remembered session persistent cookies and any other session
  browser-session cookies without `Max-Age`; every refresh keeps the choice. A remembered session
  lasts Shomei's `rememberedSessionTTL` (365 days, `SHOMEI_REMEMBERED_SESSION_TTL`), any other
  `sessionTTL` (30 days). The reference portal offers the checkbox, checked by default. See
  [the auth portal](../user/auth-portal.md#staying-signed-in) and ADR 15.

## Changed

- **The built-in `/_nagare/login` and a portal that does not send `remember` now give
  browser-session cookies** once Shomei is upgraded, where every session had persistent cookies
  before.

## Fixed

- **A new Shomei or En image no longer blocks platform plans (BUG-9).** The auth component names
  each migration Job after its image, and the planner required a lifecycle decision for the Job a
  new image replaced, which no command could give. A completed migration Job that a scope
  replacement no longer declares is now retained without a decision; it is never deleted.

## Compatibility and rollback

`transitionsFrom` adds 0.10.0. Shomei's migration `0037-remembered-sessions` adds
`shomei_sessions.remembered` with default `true`, so sessions from before the upgrade keep
persistent cookies and their 30-day lifetime. An older Shomei ignores `remember`, and the new
`nagare-access` treats a missing `remembered` as remembered, so every mix during a rollout signs
people in. Deploy a portal that sends `remember` before upgrading Shomei, or its sign-ins in
between are not remembered.

The en, Shomei and nagare-access images are operator inputs: rebuild Shomei and nagare-access
from this tag and pin their digests, or the context keeps the previous behavior.

## Validation
